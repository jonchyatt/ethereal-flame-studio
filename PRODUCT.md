# Ethereal Flame Studio / What Am I Appreciating Now

## Product register

**Primary:** operator tool for Jon.
**Secondary:** public WAIA brand surfaces consumed by viewers.

Ethereal Flame Studio is the production cockpit behind **What Am I Appreciating Now** (WAIA): a self-feeding appreciation channel that turns sourced wisdom audio into differentiated binaural remixes, immersive EFS visuals, packaged releases, and measured next-slate proposals. Jon supplies taste and approval; the system supplies the hands.

## Users

- **Operator:** Jon, who needs a calm, evidence-rich review queue and should only touch explicit curation and release approvals.
- **Audience:** people seeking centering, meditation, manifestation, and contemplative wisdom. They encounter the public domain, YouTube channel, and optional Reset Biology bridges—not the production cockpit.

## Core loop

`SOURCE -> REMIX -> CURATE -> ASSIGN -> RENDER -> PACKAGE -> PUBLISH -> LEARN`

Nothing renders or publishes without a durable approval record. Every source retains provenance and a rights basis. Engine (`puppeteer | unity`) and execution target (`cloud | home | local-agent`) are independent.

## Brand meaning

The circular WAIAN mark is a living cycle:

`DESIRE -> FULFILL -> APPRECIATE -> ALLOW -> NEXT DESIRE / GROWTH`

It has no fixed beginning or end. The visual language should feel contemplative, alive, generative, and precise—never sleepy, generic, or dashboard-corporate. Reuse the existing WAIAN ring and the public front door's night-sky, warm parchment, gold, and indigo system. Motion should imply emergence and return, with a reduced-motion equivalent.

## Voice and boundaries

- Curator voice, not Jon narration.
- No vlog drift, engagement bait, Crit Fail tone, fake spiritual claims, or invented performance numbers.
- Third-party source rights and transformation evidence stay visible to the operator without becoming audience-facing theater.
- Public actions, OAuth changes, vendor commitments, purchases, and publish sends remain explicit approval gates.
- Reset Biology integration is contextual and data-minimal; it is never automatic cross-promotion.

## Experience principles

1. Show where an artifact is in the ring and what must happen next.
2. Make the safe action obvious; explain every disabled or blocked action.
3. The UI is a projection of durable records, never a second source of truth.
4. Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels, captions/transcripts, reduced motion, and browser zoom.
5. Prefer a single operator surface over parallel dashboards.

## Business posture

Success begins with the channel paying for its own operation. Merchandise, future social adapters, and Reset Biology bridges follow a verified YouTube vertical slice and reuse the same approval, provenance, packaging, and measurement records. Do not invent vendors, pricing, inventory, cadence, or KPIs; those are operator decisions backed by measured demand.
