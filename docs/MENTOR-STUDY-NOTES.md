## Kallaway: judge a format on four things (newsletter, 2026-10-18)

Time to execution (idea to finished video, 1 to 10); visual requirement; people requirement; overall difficulty of one rep (1 to 10). His rule: pick the easiest format and visual layout and film that for the first 50 videos. His tech channel uses one format and one layout for every video; his marketing channel uses 3 to 4 formats and one layout. Adding a second core format took one member from 3 to 5K views to 40K+ on 5 of the next 7 attempts.

How this applies to What Am I Appreciating Now (my reading, not Kallaway's claim): our format needs no people and no filming, since it is mix plus EFS visuals, so the production constraint he recommends is already met. The open question is the second format (Shorts cut from each mix) and whether to hold one visual layout across the first 50 uploads. Content-shape choices stay Jonathan's (HB-4).
