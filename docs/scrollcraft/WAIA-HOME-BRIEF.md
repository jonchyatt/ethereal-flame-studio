## Step 1 answers
- **What is this, and who for?** A small studio's gratitude practices and 360° scenes for
  people who want a quiet minute, plus a few longer programs and a shelf (site tree doc).
- **What must the visitor believe by the end?** *This is a quiet place I can come back to.*
- **What does the visitor do next?** Try a practice. One label everywhere: **Try a practice**.
  (The close also offers the channel, "Watch on YouTube", as the follow-along action.)
- **Art direction.** The brand's own rendered world (orb, ocean, nebula), not photography,
  because the brand is literally this rendered place. Not clay, not illustration.

## Grammar
**Gallery / catalog** (uniqueness.md 2.6). The channel describes itself as curated pieces;
a storefront's real question is "what are the options". Nav is an index of objects that
jumps; the hero is object one, already labelled; every object carries the same label
schema; the close is an inquiry plate typeset like a label.
Why the other seven lost: filmic one-shot forbids more than one entry point and a
storefront index; chaptered editorial bans media above the fold, and Jon's complaint is
about what people see when they land; live surface is for software; continuous world needs
seam-matched chained legs we do not have and puts every link in a fixed copy layer;
typographic poster bans photographic ground and scrub, and the orb-over-water is the
look Jon asked for; split stage needs a two-sided argument; rhythmic cutlist is an energy
grammar and this is a quiet one.

Label schema (no exceptions): **Title.** / *Kind. Length or state.* / one or two plain
fact sentences / credit line / one action.

## Feeling curve (written before the acts)
1. **Wonder, quiet.** The gold orb over water is already there; the planes drift apart as you move.
2. **Intimacy.** Three small lights, each a voice; one tap to hear one.
3. **Release (PEAK).** The scroll carries you across the water toward the light while its
   ring of words turns from "What am I appreciating now?" to "Now, what am I appreciating?"
4. **Orientation.** The rest of the house, labelled plainly, nothing sold at you.
5. **Belonging, resolved.** One plain plate: the channel and every door, holding still.

## The peak
"I scrolled and drifted across the water toward the light, and its name turned around it
until the question became the answer." Lives in act 3. Largest span on the page (3.4),
the only clip on the page, a quieter act before it (a small labelled rail).

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where **you drift across a night ocean toward a glowing orb and the words
circling it turn until the question answers itself.**

## Signature move
**The hinge ring.** The five-word name set as one continuous clockwise ring around the
orb, baselines inward (upside down along the bottom, per Jon's merch rule), with a gold
reading mark at twelve o'clock. Scroll through the peak turns the ring until "NOW" reaches
the mark, and the caption under it hinges from the question to its answer. Pure CSS
reading the act's `--sc-p`; the engine is untouched. The ring's rest state follows Jon's
carved panel (WHAT at the top, APPRECIATING upside down along the bottom, NOW climbing
the left side), so the turn starts from the true mark and ends with NOW at the top.
