Quote clips and any recording without a free licence need written permission from the rights holder, and signing binds you. Built so far: the per-clip ledger with rights basis and evidence fields, and permission-request drafts. Nothing is sent or signed yet. On yes: name the clip; you review the draft and sign it yourself. On no: that clip stays out and the playlist is released without it.
